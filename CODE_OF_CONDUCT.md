# Code of Conduct

Piyo follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The short version:

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome contributing to Piyo: riders, drivers, mappers, designers and developers,
regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and direct. Assume good faith; ask before judging.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect that route data comes from people on the street; treat reports and contributions with care.
- Spanish and English are both welcome in issues and reviews.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private
information (including locations or device identifiers), and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through GitHub ("Report content" on the item, or a direct
message to a maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.
